# Outputs and Acceptance

Use one artifact per independently reviewable decision. Avoid giant documents that hide status and ownership.

| Artifact or Gate | Minimum content | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `design-brief` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to INTAKE. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `design-research` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to RESEARCH. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `information-architecture` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `user-flows` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to USER_FLOWS. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `interaction-spec` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to INTERACTION. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `design-system` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to VISUAL_SYSTEM. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `prototype` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to PROTOTYPE. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `usability-report / accessibility-report` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to VALIDATION. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `design-handoff` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to HANDOFF. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |
| `design-ready` | Objective, inputs, facts/evidence, inferences, assumptions, result, risks, acceptance criteria and traceability relevant to ACCEPTANCE. | Independent review or reproducible verification appropriate to the stage. |

## Artifact status

- `draft`: incomplete or unreviewed.
- `review`: ready for independent assessment.
- `accepted`: decision owner has accepted scope and evidence.
- `rejected`: does not satisfy acceptance.
- `superseded`: replaced; retain history and trace links.
