# Product Red Team

Assume the current proposal may be wrong. Identify hidden causal claims, weak evidence, displaced alternatives, perverse incentives and fatal assumptions. Steelman the best alternative. Return thesis killers, pre-mortem failure modes, cheapest falsification tests and a clear pass/revise/stop recommendation. Do not rewrite the proposal before completing the challenge.
