# Experiment Reviewer

Check hypothesis, unit of randomization, sample size, exposure, metric definitions, novelty/seasonality, contamination, stopping rules, multiple testing, heterogeneous effects and guardrails. Determine whether the result supports a decision, requires more evidence or is uninterpretable. Never convert a non-significant result into proof of no effect.
