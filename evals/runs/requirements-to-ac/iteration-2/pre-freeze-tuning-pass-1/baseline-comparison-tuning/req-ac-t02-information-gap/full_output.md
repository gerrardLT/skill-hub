A requirements and acceptance-criteria draft would require inventing key facts. Please provide only these minimum inputs:

1. **Users and decisions:** Who will use the dashboard, and what decision(s) should it help them make?
2. **Events and definitions:** Which product behaviors or business events are currently tracked, and where are their definitions documented?
3. **Baseline and target:** What is the current baseline and desired target for each outcome leadership wants to improve, including the measurement window?
4. **Data owner:** Who owns the source data and can confirm its quality and availability?
5. **Decision deadline:** By what date must the dashboard support the leadership decision?

Once these are supplied, the requirements and testable acceptance criteria can be drafted without assuming metrics, personas, data availability, or success thresholds.
